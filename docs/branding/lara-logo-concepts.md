# Lara Logo Concepts

This document outlines three concepts for the Lara personal intelligent agent logo.

> Adopted 2026-10-16: a variant of Concept 1 ("L" mark + circuit brain) is now the
> product logo. Master asset: `lara-logo.png` in this directory (660px, transparent
> rounded card). Derivatives: `apps/desktop/public/lara-logo.png` (startup screen +
> favicon), `apps/desktop/renderer/src/assets/lara-logo.png` (sidebar brand mark),
> `apps/desktop/electron/main/assets/lara.ico` (window/taskbar/installer icon).

## Concept 1: The Digital Spark

*   **Description:** A stylized 'L' made of connected light points or circuit lines, forming a subtle spark or brain shape.
*   **Symbolism:** Represents the "spark" of intelligence and the digital nature of the agent.
*   **Colors:** Electric blue, light green, maybe a touch of purple.
*   **Feel:** Dynamic, intelligent, energetic.

## Concept 2: The Guardian/Companion

*   **Description:** A simplified human silhouette or head profile with a glowing network or data cloud inside it.
*   **Symbolism:** Emphasizes the "personal" aspect and the agent's role as a helpful companion or guardian.
*   **Colors:** Warm blue, soft orange, white.
*   **Feel:** Trustworthy, personal, insightful.

## Concept 3: The Abstract 'L'

*   **Description:** A minimalist, geometric 'L' that incorporates a subtle arrow (progress) or a dot (focus/data).
*   **Symbolism:** Focuses on a clean, professional, and modern identity. The arrow or dot adds a layer of meaning about progress or focus.
*   **Colors:** Monochromatic or a single accent color like teal.
*   **Feel:** Professional, clean, modern.
